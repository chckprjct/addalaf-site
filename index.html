How it works

1. Set the vibe (and the ground rules).

Tell Addalaf how your humor sounds and what you'll never joke about. It remembers, for every talk. Then pick the tone, from a warm icebreaker to something with real bite.

2. Let the writers' room work.

Give it the occasion and one specific, awkward detail. Instead of generic internet jokes, Addalaf builds on what's true about your room: the reorg everyone survived, the projector that has never once worked.

3. Spin the roulette.

Get a few genuinely different angles. Keep, edit, or toss each one. You decide what goes on stage.

The fine print · lines it never crosses

Whatever the settings, Addalaf won't write jokes that demean people for who they are, jokes aimed at someone in the audience, or jokes about a loss you've told it about. When a moment calls for sincerity, it says so instead of forcing a laugh.

We are a comedy engine, not a sociopath.

FAQ

What does it cost?

It's free to start: 3 Brief rounds and 1 Script round a month. Plus is $3.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.

Is my speech private?

Your speeches stay in your account. To write suggestions, what you enter is sent to our AI providers, Google and Anthropic. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

Does it work for a eulogy?

It treats serious moments seriously. If a moment isn't one for jokes, it tells you, and why.

Is it on Android?

Addalaf is on iPhone, in the App Store. There's no Android version.

Can I edit the jokes?

Every one. (Think of us as your head writer; you still have final cut.)
